# Study context

- Informants: 17 experts working in the information systems development (ISD) industry.
- Data: semi-structured interviews of about 60 minutes, transcribed in Finnish
  (roughly 6000-8000 words each). Quotes stay in Finnish; labels are written in English.
- Unit of 1st-order coding: **technology trends and trajectories** that informants describe,
  i.e. changes over time in technologies, how software is built, or how work is done.
  A technology, tool or method merely mentioned is not a trend. Ask: what is changing,
  in which direction?
- The interview guide used ISD lifecycle models (e.g. waterfall, spiral model, Scrum) to frame
  the discussion. Mentioning them because the interviewer asked is not a trend; code them only
  when the informant describes a change in their use.
- Informants are knowledgeable agents (Gioia): report their views, do not judge them.
- Keep the specific meaning. Example from the human analysis: "dominance of a few cloud
  services" captures both cloud platforms and vendor lock-in; "cloud" alone loses that.
